FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I migrate from AWS, GCP or Azure?

Yes. Our migration toolkit imports containers, databases and DNS, and our solutions team offers free migration assistance on Pro and Enterprise plans.

Which languages and frameworks are supported?

Anything that runs in a container. We auto-detect Node.js, Python, Go, Rust, Ruby, Java, PHP and .NET, plus frameworks like Next.js, Django, Rails and Spring.

How does usage-based billing work?

You pay a flat per-seat fee plus metered compute, bandwidth and storage beyond the included amounts. Spending limits and alerts keep surprises away.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Voltic is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR and HIPAA compliant.
